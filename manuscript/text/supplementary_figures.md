![Feasibility and conditional boundary-to-interior ALAN profiles for protected areas in 2024. The left panel shows ring feasibility: the fraction of protected areas meeting the minimum-pixel rule (>= 10 valid pixels) within each interior ring (0-1 km, 1-5 km, 5-10 km). Feasibility declines sharply with interior distance (approximately 39.8% for 0-1 km, 12.8% for 1-5 km, and 3.4% for 5-10 km), reflecting small protected area sizes at 500 m resolution. The right panel shows a conditional edge-penetration diagnostic (`frac_gt0`) restricted to protected areas with complete ring coverage (n = 383). Median `frac_gt0` is zero under floor domination, so the conditional non-zero share should not be interpreted as proof of either presence or absence of an edge effect.](../figures/fig_layerA_edge_profiles_aggregate.png){#fig-layerA-edge-profiles-s1 width=90%}

![Uncertainty in inside-protected-area brightening trends using an IBRA-clustered bootstrap. Inside mean radiance trends were estimated as Theil-Sen slopes for 2013-2024 and bootstrapped using IBRA regions as spatial clusters and a moving-block resample of years (block length 3 years; n_boot = 300). The figure shows the top protected areas by median bootstrap slope with bootstrap intervals and prob(slope > 0), illustrating that a small number of sites remain robustly positive while many ranks are weakly separated under dependence-aware resampling.](../figures/fig_layerA_trend_uncertainty_forest.png){#fig-layerA-trend-uncertainty-s2 width=90%}

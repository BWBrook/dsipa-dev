![Full-period inside Theil–Sen slopes and annual variation among zero-slope areas. Of the 4,426 eligible protected areas, 420 have positive, 37 negative and 3,969 exactly zero slopes, and the distribution of non-zero slopes is shown in full, without clipping its tails. All zero-slope series had 12 valid years: 3,786 were zero throughout, three had constant non-zero values and 180 varied from year to year. Seventy-eight of the varying series were zero in 2013–2021 and positive in at least one of 2022–2024. A zero full-period slope therefore need not imply an unchanging annual series.](../figures/fig_layerA_trend_distribution.png){#fig-layerA-trend-distribution width=100%}

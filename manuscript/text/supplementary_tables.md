**Table S1. Selected protected areas with full-period slopes and marginal temporal sensitivity.**

Each set contains the first 20 entries in its preserved source order. Sets A, B and C are ordered by descending inside slope, surrounding slope, and inside-minus-surrounding difference, respectively. Set A requires a positive inside slope; B a positive surrounding and non-positive inside slope; C a positive inside slope exceeding the surrounding slope. These are illustrative high-magnitude subsets of those metrics, not management priorities. All slopes and ranges use scaled product units per year. The range is the 2.5th-97.5th percentiles of the independently resampled inside slopes, not a confidence interval. The final column is the fraction of those slopes greater than zero; the denominator varies with IBRA selection (Supporting Methods S1). Full identifiers, IBRA assignments, areas and draw counts are retained in the accompanying source table. A dagger marks a surrounding comparison that is provisional because the site is near a documented coastline-source exception; local map validation is required. Inside slopes and temporal resampling are unchanged by this flag.

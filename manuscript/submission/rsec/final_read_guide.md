# RSEC private reading guide — 19 September 2026

Read `build/final_read/spatial_20260919/dsipa_rsec_main.pdf` and its Supporting
Information. Editable Word versions accompany both PDFs. Earlier reading bundles
are preserved. Barry remains the sole human author.

The corrected analysis uses unsimplified kilometre buffers and inward bands,
with AIMS Coast50K2024 land polygons. All whole-reserve annual observations remain
unchanged. There are 4,253 paired comparisons: 347 both positive, 70 inside-only,
3,587 neither positive and 249 surroundings-only. Compared with the previous
implementation, 407 reserves enter, 12 leave, and 57 common cases change category.
Henderson now has a positive surrounding slope and remains an explicit example.

The matched interior cohort contains 1,341 reserves. Of its 150 positive
whole-reserve trends, 42 remain positive in the 1–5 km band; 100 have a positive
outer-kilometre slope and zero deeper-band slope. Eligibility and sign agreement
against the earlier implementation are reported separately in Table S5.

Nine surrounding comparisons near known coastline-source exceptions carry
provisional flags in `reserve_screening_results.csv` and require local map
validation. Excluding them leaves 4,244 pairs and category counts 341/70/3,585/248.
These flags affect site interpretation, not the unchanged inside observations.

The 2017 first-positive pulse, 2023 increases, observed ten-unit pixel floor,
and unresolved processing-versus-environmental attribution remain explicit.
The study supports conservation screening, not causal attribution, ecological
darkness certification or an intervention priority ranking.

Optional numerical Layer B results are omitted from this candidate because its
original occurrence records could not be recovered to verify a sampling repair.
The historical research code, reports and previous paper versions are preserved.

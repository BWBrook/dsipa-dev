# Literature Review Background

Note: this is background material and context for the `docs/` folder. It is not part of the manuscript text. The references are intentionally retained as plain-text citation details for now; convert to BetterBibTeX/CSL tokens only in a later reference pass.

## 1. Longcore & Rich 2004 - "ecological light pollution" coined and scoped (foundational framing)

**Citation details**

- **Longcore, T. & Rich, C.** 2004. *Ecological light pollution.* Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment.
- **DOI:** 10.1890/1540-9295(2004)002[0191:ELP]2.0.CO;2.

**What it does / why it matters**

- Defines ALAN as a form of pollution with ecological consequences across taxa and processes (still the cleanest high-level frame).

**How it connects to this paper**

- **Introduction (problem statement / terminology)** and **Discussion (why darkness is a conservation asset).**

## 2. Hölker et al. 2010 - transdisciplinary research and policy agenda (why this is not "just aesthetics")

**Citation details**

- **Hölker, F. et al.** 2010. *The dark side of light: a transdisciplinary research agenda for light pollution policy.* Ecology and Society.
- **DOI:** 10.5751/ES-03685-150413.

**What it does / why it matters**

- Positions light pollution as a genuine environmental management problem with monitoring and policy needs.

**How it connects to this paper**

- **Introduction (policy relevance)** and **Discussion (management implications for protected areas).**

## 3. Gaston et al. 2013 - mechanistic synthesis of ecological impacts (the "how" behind patterns)

**Citation details**

- **Gaston, K.J. et al.** 2013. *The ecological impacts of nighttime light pollution: a mechanistic appraisal.* Biological Reviews.
- **DOI:** 10.1111/brv.12036.

**What it does / why it matters**

- Organises impacts by mechanisms (behaviour, physiology, trophic interactions), which is exactly what we need when interpreting signal vs artefact.

**How it connects to this paper**

- **Introduction (mechanistic motivation)** and **Discussion (linking ALAN metrics to plausible ecological pathways).**

## 4. Sanders et al. 2021 - meta-analysis of ALAN biological impacts (state-of-evidence anchor)

## Design amendment to correspondence message 193

Fable proposed distance to any positive 2013–2021 pixel and comparison of
2015–2021 versus 2022 first-detection hazards. That definition includes the
historical outcomes in their own distance strata: earlier detections can make
themselves near-light. It cannot provide an unbiased historical far-light
reference. Before inspecting the new outputs, replace it with distance to
positive **2013** raster cells, held fixed throughout 2014–2024. Record this as a
prospective amendment, not as Fable's original prespecification.

Distance is the minimum from each reserve polygon to a positive raster-cell
centre in the 2013 layer, measured in EPSG:3577. Strata are <2, 2–20 and >20 km
(20 belongs to the middle stratum). This measures proximity to baseline product
detection, not roads, subsequent development or independent environmental
exposure. Cell-centre distances have finite raster resolution. Unsupported
spatial coverage remains unclassified. Any implementation adjustment must be
recorded before interpreting the resulting comparison.

## Comparisons

1. Read matching Australian raster windows for 2021 and 2022, with 2013 and
   2024 context. Retain stored integer and metadata-adjusted value distributions,
   valid/negative/no-data counts, scale/offset, and positive-pixel transitions.
   A coarser representable quantum does not itself establish a detection
   threshold or prove that all first detections arise from masking.
2. Use the accepted annual inside and 1/5 km unprotected-land buffer tables.
   Apply the original >=10 valid-pixel annual rule. Primary descriptive hazards
   use complete 12-year series with a zero 2013 baseline, avoiding unobserved
   prior detections and changing coverage. For each year, the denominator is
   zones with every preceding value zero; the numerator is their first positive
   annual mean. Report counts, denominators and fractions separately by zone.
   Report exclusions and stratify inside hazards using the fixed 2013 distance.
3. For first detections in 2022, report mean/maximum/positive-pixel-fraction
   distributions and 2023/2024 persistence or reversion. Zone means depend on
   reserve size and cannot identify the positive pixel quantum. Compare the
   same signatures for other first-detection years where useful.
4. Compare the far-stratum 2022 hazard with the 2015–2021 range and report
   2023/2024 separately, always showing the small-count denominators. Treat a
   far-stratum excess as a compatibility warning, not proof of processing:
   real isolated development can occur, while processing effects can depend on
   prior brightness. Near-stratum concentration cannot validate environmental
   encroachment. Persistence and intermittency likewise do not uniquely identify
   cause. No p-value or causal classification follows from these screens.
5. If lineage and stored-input diagnostics leave environmental interpretation
   ambiguous, seek a small matched external-product comparison under Barry's
   authorisation. Distinguish common sensor inputs from independently processed
   observations, harmonise footprint/quality/units, and report access limits.

limits, whether or not it supports environmental change. Preserve the 78 as
observed late-positive series unless the accepted-table reconstruction fails.
Do not label them new lighting without corroboration. Put product-boundary

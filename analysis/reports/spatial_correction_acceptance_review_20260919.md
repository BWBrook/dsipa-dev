## Scientific target and scope of inference

The target is a descriptive national screen of annual, product-scaled VIIRS-derived radiance for the fixed CAPAD 2024 protected-area estate over 2013–2024. Its main estimands are per-PA Theil–Sen slopes of the whole-reserve mean and the mean in a 1 km surrounding support defined as AIMS Coast50K2024 land minus the complete protected-area estate. Q1–Q4 classify the signs of these two point estimates. Inward bands are conditional spatial diagnostics. There is no population, causal, intervention, ecological-response, or site-priority estimand and no out-of-sample prediction target.

The observation process selects raster-cell centres within fixed polygons, decodes each annual raster's scale metadata, requires at least 10 valid cells per zone-year and eight usable years per slope, and retains zeros as product non-detections. The resulting frequency statements apply to the eligible geometry-selected cohort, not to all 11,133 identifiers as exchangeable ecological replicates. The manuscript now makes these limits explicit, distinguishes product radiance from illumination or sky brightness, treats the 2024 estate and coastline as fixed retrospective supports, and does not use the marginal block resampling as calibrated confidence intervals.

### MINOR — nine coastline comparisons remain locally unvalidated, but the national result is robust to their exclusion

Nineteen PA identifiers fall within 5 km of documented coastline-source exception points; nine enter the paired cohort. Swan River Management Area (`WA_48325`) and Brisbane Water National Park (`NSW_N0006`) newly gain Q1 eligibility. The bounded map diagnostic places the documented points 1.39 km and 0.42 km from the respective 1 km supports, and neither point intersects a support, but this cannot certify shoreline truth or rule out a broader mapping error.

The adopted treatment is defensible for a screening product: all 19 identifiers have a machine-readable proximity flag, the nine paired results are marked `provisional_surrounding_comparison`, the manuscript requires local map validation before site use, and the reserve-level table carries the status. Excluding all nine leaves 4,244 paired reserves with Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 counts of 341/70/3,585/248, compared with 347/70/3,587/249 in the complete cohort. Thus the headline national pattern does not depend on these sites. Local authoritative shoreline or orthoimage review is the evidence needed to promote either newly eligible site from provisional to site-valid.

### MINOR — computational replication is strong but does not include an independent second national extraction

The sealed run contains all 928 completion manifests, 4,640 verified part files, exact code/input/runtime contracts and the executed runner and engine snapshots. A fresh public-baseline run reproduced every field for 576 buffer rows and 288 ring rows across eight deliberately varied reserves, including coastal examples and Wilinggin. Public and accepted baseline tables and geometries were separately shown semantically identical. This is adequate evidence for the private candidate because the national run is deterministic and its complete intermediate hashes verify clean assembly. A second full national extraction from public inputs would be the strongest release-level replication, but it is not required to interpret the present results and would add cost without targeting a remaining discrepancy.

### MINOR — residual scientific limits are correctly presented as limits, not modelled uncertainty

Eligibility is informative with respect to PA size and shape; overlapping designations are not independent; annual values and neighbouring PAs are spatially and temporally correlated; strict sign categories have no minimum-effect threshold; and the marginal temporal resampling does not propagate coastline, boundary, raster, product-version, or model-selection uncertainty. Product zeros combine true low signal with upstream masking and quantisation. These conditions preclude calibrated category probabilities, population prevalence, formal multiple-testing statements, or causal attribution. They do not invalidate a descriptive screen. The manuscript now limits inference accordingly and reports annual series, denominators, magnitude summaries and spatial sensitivities rather than interpreting all zero slopes as no change.

## Evidence and diagnostics

The old Natural Earth mask is retained only as a geometry-only comparator. The fixed 2024 supports estimate change on the 2024 monitoring estate, not the effect of designation or historical boundary change.

Three 5 km raw supports differed by one selected cell; no 1 km or inward support, eligibility decision or sign changed. Two affected annual histories were all zero; the third slope changed by 0.023%. Comparing the preserved extraction file with the canonical file showed exact equality of every original field; the sole new column is the constant `0.3.0`.

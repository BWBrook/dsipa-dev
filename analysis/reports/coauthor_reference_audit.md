# Cited-reference correction, 2026-09-12

The current main manuscript and Supporting Methods cite 24 records: 22 journal
articles and two Zenodo datasets. Crossref CSL metadata was retrieved for all
22 journal DOIs and DataCite/Zenodo records checked for the datasets. Structured
family/given names were retained, including compound surnames. The resulting
DOIs and author-list lengths were reconciled to `references/refs.yaml`.

  reductions in star visibility; retained its correct DOI.
- Linley: DOI `10.1071/AM15047` identifies the 2017 article on lighting and bats
  along native coastal vegetation. Corrected title/year and changed the cited
  key from `linley2016` to `linley2017`.
- Mu 2021: corrected title and first-author given name (Haowei).
- Baker 2024: corrected first-author given name (David), completed title and
  co-authors.

Other cited journal records received complete author lists and available
volume, issue and page/article fields. The evidence is retained in
`coauthor_reference_metadata.json`. Uncited historical bibliography entries
were outside this bounded check.

Citation placement was also corrected: Garrett supports skyglow in
conservation-priority areas rather than a temporal-increase claim; Lewanzik
supports biological responses to lighting conversion; presence-only bias
papers support sampling-process limitations; Roberts supports spatially
structured evaluation. The recommended repeated-survey design is presented
as the authors' recommendation.
